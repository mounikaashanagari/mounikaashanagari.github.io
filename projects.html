01 · Master’s thesis · 2026

Integrating Alzheimer’s genetics with single-nucleus transcriptomics

Integrated Wightman and Bellenguez Alzheimer’s disease GWAS with ROSMAP prefrontal-cortex single-nucleus transcriptomics. Used MAGMA, scDRS and CELLEX to prioritize CNS-associated macrophages and microglia, then applied scNET to compare network modules with SEA-AD.
